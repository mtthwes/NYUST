Singapore

Singapore, officially known as the Republic of Singapore, is a city-state and island country in Southeast Asia. The capital city of Singapore is Singapore City.

Country Information

  • Capital: Singapore City
  • President: Halima Jacob
  • Language: English, Chinese, Malay
  • Population: More than 5.63 million

History

Singapore's history can be traced back to the 11th century. It gained importance as a port in the 14th century under the Malacca Sultanate. The modern history of Singapore began in 1819 when Stamford Raffles, an Englishman, established the island as a port. Under British rule, Singapore flourished as a major seaport city, facilitating trade between India, China, and Southeast Asia. After gaining independence, Singapore focused on modernization, developing light industry, national housing, and investing heavily in public education. Today, Singapore is one of the most prosperous countries in the world.

GDP

The total GDP of Singapore is about 757.726 billion US dollars.

Festivals

Singapore celebrates various festivals including National Day, Hari Raya, Vesak, and Eid al-Adha, among others. These festivals showcase the diverse cultural heritage of the country.
