Philippines

The Philippines, located in the Western Pacific Ocean, is a multi-ethnic archipelago in Southeast Asia. Composed of more than 7,100 islands, it is often referred to as "the country of a thousand islands." The capital city of the Philippines is Manila.

Country Information

  • Capital: Manila
  • President: Ferdinand Romualdez Marcos Jr.（小斐迪南·羅慕爾德茲·馬可仕）
  • Language: Filipino and English
  • Population: More than 113.9 million

History

The Philippines has a rich history influenced by various cultures. It was ruled by Spain for 356 years, which deeply influenced the country's culture. The name "Philippines" originated from the Spanish name "Las Islas Filipinas," named after Prince Philip of Spain. The Philippines has experienced periods of rapid economic growth and has been referred to as one of the Four Asian Tigers. The country is known for its fusion of Eastern and Western cultures due to long-term colonization and commercial activities.

Today, the Philippines has a mixed-race population, and the majority of Filipinos are devout Catholics. English is widely spoken, with over 60% to 70% of the population understanding the language. The Philippines consistently ranks high in happiness surveys and is known for the cheerful and hospitable nature of its people.

GDP

The GDP of the Philippines in 2021 was 394.1 billion USD.

Festivals

The Philippines celebrates a variety of vibrant festivals, including the Ati-Atihan Festival, Sinulog Festival, Panagbenga Festival (Baguio Flower Festival), Moriones Festival, Pahiyas Festival, Parada ng Lechon, Sandugo Festival, Kadayawan Festival (Kadayawan sa Dabaw), MassKara Festival, and Christmas.
